About Foldra Studio

Considered in every fold.

Foldra Studio designs, sources and delivers packaging that protects your product and makes every delivery feel special.

01Who we are

Your packaging is often the first thing a customer holds from your brand. We make that moment count.

We learn what your product is, how it sells and how it travels. We recommend the right formats and materials, design them, and source them with our production partners. You approve a physical sample. Then we deliver packaging your team can use straight away.

Box suppliers sell you a box. Agencies design one and leave you to find a maker. We design the packaging and manage sourcing, sampling and production with our partners, so you deal with one studio from brief to delivery. Our studio is in Mumbai, and our clients can be anywhere.

Mission

Help brands protect their products, present them well and make every delivery feel considered.

Vision

The first name a growing Indian brand thinks of when its product needs packaging.

Our promise

Considered in every fold: the right packaging, at the agreed price, on the agreed date.

Every product carries a promise. Every delivery is a chance to make someone feel valued. Your product delivers the value; your packaging helps your customer feel it.

What we believe

02Our values

Four things we hold to on every job.

i.Precision

Exact sizes, clean folds, packaging built to fit the product.

ii.Craft

Material and finish are part of the design, not an add-on.

iii.Responsibility

Right-sized packaging and recyclable paper-based options where they suit the product. Plastic only when it does a job nothing else can.

iv.Clarity

Plain answers on cost, lead time and options, including the trade-offs.
